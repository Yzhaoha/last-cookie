## Title
Last Cookie

## Short description（一句话）
One cookie, eight hungry snacks. Grab it, run, and eat it before anyone bumps it out of your hands!

## Description
Last Cookie is a fast party brawler on a breakfast table. There is only ONE cookie and everybody wants it.

- Grab the cookie and run — while you hold it, you are eating it. The first snack to finish it (100%) wins.
- Bump whoever is holding it to steal it. Jump to dodge their bumps.
- Eat chocolate chips to grow and unlock new moves: a longer dash, a ground slam, and a shield.
- Pick up chili (speed), ice (freeze everyone nearby) and bubbles (block one hit). Watch out for sticky jam!
- Knock rivals off the table — but don't get knocked off yourself.
- Two tables, daily gifts and quests, and 19 skins to unlock from mystery boxes.

Quick matches of about 90 seconds. Works on desktop and mobile.

## Controls
Desktop
- Move: WASD or arrow keys
- Bump / steal: Space or K
- Jump / dodge: J or Shift
- Pause: P

Mobile
- Move: drag anywhere on the screen
- Bump and Jump: the two buttons
